How to Use It

1

Download the QR code image above.

2

Print it on a laminated card or sticker — credit card size works great.

3

Place it on your dashboard or windshield where it's visible.

4

When someone scans it, their phone opens your contact page showing a masked number and a call button — your real number stays hidden from the page but is embedded in the call link so the call connects.
